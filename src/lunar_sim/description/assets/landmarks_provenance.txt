Landmark rock assets
====================

description/assets/rocks/landmark_0.stl .. landmark_6.stl and landmark_mesa.stl are
procedural meshes, not scans of lunar samples. Their source is
description/generate_landmarks.py, copyright 2026 PickNik Inc., BSD-3-Clause
(see the source header and src/lunar_sim/LICENSE). Each is the convex hull of a
seeded point cloud with planar fracture cuts shaved off; two are weathered by
pulling their corners in, and the mesa is an irregular stepped outline.

Source URL:
https://github.com/PickNikRobotics/moveit_pro_example_ws/blob/main/src/lunar_sim/description/generate_landmarks.py
Mesh directory:
https://github.com/PickNikRobotics/moveit_pro_example_ws/tree/main/src/lunar_sim/description/assets/rocks

The meshes were generated with numpy 1.26.4 and scipy 1.11.4; a different
qhull may order hull vertices differently, so regenerating elsewhere can rewrite
the STLs without changing the shapes.

landmarks_assets_generated.xml records each rock's scale and
landmarks_geoms_generated.xml its placement. Heights are sampled from the
committed collision heightmaps (lunar_hfield.png and the lunar_band_*.png
strips), with the lower part of each rock buried. No geometry is generated at
runtime.

The material reuses lunar_regolith_untiled.png, whose source is nine public
domain NASA Apollo 15/17 photographs. See ground_colormap_provenance.txt for
the frame identifiers, crops and processing steps. This surface
is a regolith-covered approximation, not a rock-specific photo texture.
