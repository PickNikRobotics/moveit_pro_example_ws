Boulder assets
==============

The sixteen large boulders reuse rocks/rock_0.stl through rocks/rock_5.stl,
already committed in this package. No new mesh or texture is introduced.
These shapes are procedural assets, not scans of lunar samples. Their source
is description/generate_terrain.py, copyright 2026 PickNik Inc., BSD-3-Clause
(see the source header and src/lunar_sim/LICENSE).

Source URL:
https://github.com/PickNikRobotics/moveit_pro_example_ws/blob/main/src/lunar_sim/description/generate_terrain.py
Mesh directory:
https://github.com/PickNikRobotics/moveit_pro_example_ws/tree/main/src/lunar_sim/description/assets/rocks

boulders_assets.xml applies scales of 0.6 to 1.3, some non-uniform, to the
existing unit-radius meshes. boulders_geoms.xml records the placements.
Centers are sampled from the committed lunar_hfield.png, with the lower part
of each boulder buried. No geometry is generated at runtime.

The material reuses lunar_regolith_untiled.png, whose source is nine public
domain NASA Apollo 15/17 photographs. See ground_colormap_provenance.txt for
the frame identifiers, crops and processing steps. This surface
is a regolith-covered approximation, not a new rock-specific photo texture.
