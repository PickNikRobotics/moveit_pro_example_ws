# Asset sources

Where the physically based rendering (PBR) textures, skyboxes and retextured meshes in the simulation configs come from. Poly Haven assets are CC0 1.0 (<https://polyhaven.com/license>), which requires no attribution; they are credited here anyway. Each texture set is stored as 1024 × 1024 PNGs (2048 × 2048 for grinding_sim's `dirt_ground`): a colour map, a normal map, and an `_orm` map packing ambient occlusion, roughness and metalness into its three channels, as MuJoCo's `orm` material layer expects. The rest were generated by PickNik.
